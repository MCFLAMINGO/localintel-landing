Wallet & payouts
Where your money goes when AI agents route orders or jobs through your listing.
What is a wallet?
A free digital address — like a bank account number — that receives payments automatically. No bank required. You create one once and keep it forever.
What is pathUSD?
pathUSD is a stable digital dollar — always worth $1.00 USD. When an agent routes an order to you, payment settles in pathUSD instantly. You can convert it to regular dollars anytime.
Do I need to fund it?
No upfront funding required to receive payments. You only need a small amount of gas (a few cents) to move funds out. LocalIntel covers routing fees for confirmed transactions.
How to get a free wallet in 60 seconds
1. Go to metamask.io or download the MetaMask app on your phone.
2. Create a new wallet — write down your 12-word backup phrase and store it safely. This is your only recovery option.
3. Copy your wallet address — it starts with 0x and is 42 characters long.
4. Paste it in the field below and hit Save. That's it — you're ready to receive payments.
Payment flow: Customer places order via AI agent → Surge processes payment → pathUSD sent instantly to your wallet → you convert to dollars via any exchange (Coinbase, Kraken, etc.) or spend directly where accepted.
