What professionals are asking

From realtors to contractors, here's what local intelligence actually looks like in the field.

Realtor

"Which ZIP codes in St. Johns County have the most new business permits filed in the last 6 months?"

Answer preview
ZIP 32092 (World Golf Village) leads with 47 new commercial permits. ZIP 32082 follows with 31. Both show strong Q1 momentum in services and professional trades.
Healthcare

"Are there any urgent care gaps in the Nocatee area? I'm evaluating a clinic expansion."

Answer preview
Nocatee (32081) has 2 active urgent care facilities for a population of ~28,000. Florida average is 1 per 10,000. Gap identified.
Retail

"What retail categories are underserved on the Southside of Jacksonville?"

Answer preview
Home goods, specialty food, and fitness studios show fewer than half the density of comparable Northside corridors. Pet services also below avg.
Construction

"How many active licensed roofing contractors are operating in Duval County right now?"

Answer preview
284 active roofing licenses in Duval as of today. 61 were added in the last 12 months — up 27% year-over-year, likely post-Ian response.
Restaurant

"What restaurant categories have the highest closure rate in Ponte Vedra Beach?"

Answer preview
Fine dining shows a 34% 3-year closure rate in 32082. Fast-casual and café concepts are outperforming — avg tenure 4.1 years vs 1.8 for full-service.
